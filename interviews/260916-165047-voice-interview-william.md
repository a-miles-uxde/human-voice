# Voice Interview — William

This document is a cleaned transcript of a voice-calibration interview conducted with William between 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-23. The 105-question session moved through 10 modules of survey-style and open-ended questions, plus a set of writing samples, to build a writing-voice profile. The resulting profile classified William as an "academic_technical" writer — a moderately casual, analytically precise voice that favors directness over diplomacy, with a prose style that scores more structured and analytically dense than William's own self-description suggests.

**A:** There is a place near Snoqualmie that's right off the highway, down one of the numerous side roads in the middle of nowhere. It seemed like it had been a maintained campground for large groups once upon a time except there wasn't more than the one large spot. You would have to drive down this dirt "road" that was perilous for trucks and dangerous for smaller cars that would lead directly to a loop that had two or three smaller spots and a massive central area. It was a place that promised adventure, or a good time the second you turned on that unassuming side road to get there.
